## Mellifera 0.3.1

> ⚠️ **Still early and still buggy.** This is a work-in-progress release: expect rough edges, unbalanced numbers and things that break. Do not use it on a world you care about, and please report anything you hit.

A small one: it updates what the mod is built against and fixes a hole in the Cable added in 0.3.0.

### Updated

- **NeoForge 26.2.0.76**, up from 26.2.0.56-beta - which was the last beta build of the 26.2 line. Everything from .57 on ships without the suffix, so this is twenty builds and one step of stability later. `mellifera` now requires 26.2.0.76 or newer.
- **JEI 30.30.0.205**, up from 30.16.0.134. Optional as always.

### Fixes

- **The Cable's collar was hollow.** The brass rim where a cable clamps onto a machine is an eight-by-eight box standing proud of a six-by-six pipe, and it was missing the face at the back of it. The pipe covers the middle of that face but not the one-pixel rim, so from most angles you could see straight into the box. Both collars on the item in your hand had the same hole.

### Internal

- The JEI pages no longer use the three API calls JEI has marked for removal - `RecipeType`, `addItemStack` and `addIngredient`. Nothing about the pages changes; the mod simply will not break on the day those go away.
- `README.md` is no longer hard-wrapped at 98 columns, and `DESCRIPTION.md` joins it in the repository so the mod page text stops drifting from what the mod actually does.
